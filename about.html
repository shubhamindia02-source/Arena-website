About

OUR
STORY

This year, Arena Animation proudly completes 3 Decades of excellence in vocational training, marking 30 Years of innovation, skill development, and career transformation. Since our inception in 1996, we have been at the forefront of the Media and Entertainment Education industry, empowering thousands of students with job-ready skills.

As India moves towards becoming a global creative and digital hub, Arena Animation continues to play a key role in building a skilled workforce in animation, VFX, gaming, and digital design. Our commitment to nation-building aims at helping students achieve financial independence and create successful careers for themselves in the AVGC-XR industry

Established by Aptech Limited in 1996, Arena Animation is a pioneer, trendsetter, and global leader in non-formal vocational education. With strong industry affiliations, our constantly evolving curriculum ensures students are job-ready and secure careers in top animation studios, gaming companies, and media houses worldwide. Arena students are placed as Graphic Designers, Web Designers, 2D/3D Animators, 2D/3D Designers, AV Editors, Technical Trainers, 3D Modelers, Multimedia Programmers, Compositors, Visualizers, Content Developers and Pre and Post Production Executives in the Global Media and Entertainment Industry
